Hall 02

Premium Hall 02

Traditional grandeur for weddings, muhurthams, sangeets and big family functions.

A long, open aisle runs beneath circular ceiling coves and crystal chandeliers to a golden sculpted relief wall. Patterned walls, lantern sconces and hand-painted murals, including a Ganesha relief and a forest landscape, give the hall a festive, cultural character, while sofa lounges at the front keep elders comfortable.
