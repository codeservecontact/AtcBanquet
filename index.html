Introducing ATC

Kukatpally’s address for grace & grandeur

ATC Banquets is a premium celebration venue on KPHB Main Road, built for families and businesses who want their event to feel effortless.

Backlit onyx panels, sculpted ceilings, marble flooring and a regal bridal stage set the scene. Behind the scenes, our in-house kitchen and decor team take care of the menu and styling, so you can enjoy being a guest at your own celebration.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can ATC’s banquet halls accommodate?

Each of our two premium halls comfortably hosts 200 – 350 guests. Share your guest count and seating style and we’ll recommend the right hall and layout.

Where is ATC Banquets located?

We are at Pillar No. 766 on KPHB Main Road, A.S. Raju Nagar, Kukatpally, Hyderabad 500072, easily reachable from KPHB Colony, Miyapur, Moosapet and Nizampet.

Do you provide catering and decoration?

Yes. Catering and decor are handled in-house by our own team, so food, stage, lighting and table styling are coordinated under one roof.

How do I check availability and pricing?

Send the enquiry form, call or WhatsApp us with your date, event type and guest count. We’ll confirm availability and share a quotation based on your menu and decor choices.
