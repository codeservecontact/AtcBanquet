About ATC

A premium venue with a personal touch

ATC Banquets is part of Hotel ATC on KPHB Main Road, Kukatpally: two premium banquet halls, a dedicated dining hall and an in-house team for food and decor.

We host weddings, engagements, receptions, birthdays and corporate events for 200 – 350 guests per hall. Families choose us because everything is handled in one place, and because our team treats every function as if it were our own.

  • Two air-conditioned premium halls
  • Gilded bridal & reception stages
  • In-house multi-cuisine catering
  • In-house decor & lighting
  • Lounge seating for elders & VIPs
  • Central KPHB Main Road location
Our Story

Rooted in tradition, designed for today

ATC began with a simple idea: Kukatpally’s families deserved a celebration space as special as their occasions.

The heritage mural at our entrance, with its Bharatanatyam dancer, peacocks and temple gopuram, reflects the culture we celebrate every day. Inside the halls the same spirit continues in a golden sculpted relief wall and hand-painted Ganesha and forest murals, paired with contemporary luxury: crystal chandeliers, backlit onyx and marble.

Over the years our halls have hosted muhurthams and receptions, first birthdays and golden anniversaries, product launches and team celebrations. Each one has taught us that great events come down to the details, and to people who care about them.
